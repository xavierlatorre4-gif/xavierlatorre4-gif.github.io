YT Growth readonly

YT Growth readonly is a private, read-only tool used only by its owner, Xavier Latorre Arribas, to review performance data for his own YouTube channels. No login is required to view this page.

What the app does

It reads channel and video statistics (such as views, impressions, click-through rate, watch time and video titles) so the owner can decide how to improve titles, thumbnails and publishing schedules.

Google data it accesses

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly – read the owner's channel and video metadata.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly – read the owner's YouTube Analytics reports.

The app never uploads, edits, deletes or publishes anything. Data is not shared with third parties and is never sold.
