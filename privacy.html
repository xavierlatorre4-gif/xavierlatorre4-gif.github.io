Privacy Policy – YT Growth readonly

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. Who we are

YT Growth readonly ("the app") is a private, personal tool operated by Xavier Latorre Arribas and used only for his own YouTube channels.

2. Data accessed

With the user's consent through Google OAuth, the app requests only these read-only scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: channel and video metadata (channel ID, video IDs, titles, publish dates, public statistics).
  • yt-analytics.readonly: YouTube Analytics reports (views, impressions, click-through rate, watch time, audience retention).

The app does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google data, and cannot modify any YouTube content.

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to generate performance reports and recommendations for the owner's own channels. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training general AI/ML models.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and downloaded reports are stored locally on the owner's own device and private working environment. They are not uploaded to public servers. Access is protected by the owner's device and account security.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is not transferred to anyone except as required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Reports are kept only as long as needed for analysis and are deleted by the owner when no longer useful. Deleting the local files and revoking access removes all stored data.

7. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

YT Growth readonly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
